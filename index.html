ALL THE HAINTSOF HADES

a staged reading

by Mallory Main

translated and adapted from Seneca’s Oedipus

10.16.26 · 7:00 p.m.

cast

EDDIE

Fraser Dickson

FRASER DICKSON (Eddie) is thrilled to join this talented cast in interpreting such a beautiful rendering of the Oedipus story. Fraser’s most recent Off-Off Broadway appearance was as Claudius in Hamlet (Gallery Players), and he can next be seen as Edgar/Poor Tom in King Lear (The Secret Theatre, Nov. 2027) and Bobby in David Mamet's American Buffalo (Blue Bridge Theatre, Jan 2027).

JO

Hilarie Spangler

Hilarie Spangler is an Appalachian musician, music director, teaching artist, and creative arts therapist. Hilarie has served as music director for productions throughout New York City, Nashville, and Louisville and was the Co-Founding Artistic Director of Cardinal Cross Arts Collective. Her recent work, Borrowed Ground, is a 2026 Petri Project with The TEAM.

SAMANTHA / CORA

Claudia Minor Troyer

Claudia Minor Troyer (Samantha/Cora) is thrilled to support Appalachian voices in theatre via the first public reading of this dynamic new translation. Recent credits: Very Fine People: The Battle of Charlottesville (La Mama ETC), A Thousand Stonewalls (La Mama ETC), and Five Dollar Wrench (short film). claudiaminortroyer.com

CRAVEN / DENNIS

Christopher Cosmo

Christopher Cosmo (Craven/Dennis) is a New York based actor. He has spent the past several years honing his acting skills and spending as much time on film sets and in theater environments as possible. Christopher is also a skilled ceramicist and writer. He's always looking for ways to enhance and embrace his creative energies.

R Fenstermacher is an actor, designer, Marylander in the Big Apple, illustrator, history buff, and banjo player. You can find him volunteering aboard ships, painting portraits, or giving talks on polar exploration, Elizabethan clothing, or one of the World Wars. Not all at once, though.

creative team

Director, Adapter, & Translator

Mallory Main

Mallory Main is a PhD candidate in Classics at NYU. All the Haints of Hades is their first work written for the stage. Previous directing credits include productions of Shakespeare’s Titus Andronicus, Coriolanus, The Tempest, and Hamlet in Lexington, Kentucky and Aristophanes’ Clouds in Tucson, Arizona.
